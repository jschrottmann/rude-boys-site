The Northstar method

One living document for the whole team, written so anyone can act on it.

Every engagement starts with a Northstar workshop. Owners and team leads sit down and write where the business is going, how they will know, and what great looks like on the floor. Then we build to it.

Adapted from the Northstar framework by Highland (Scot Chisholm). Rude Boys runs it for owners, for first-time founders and for our own venues.

  1. 1

    Mission and values

    Part 1, Purpose

    A 100-year mission, today’s focus in one sentence, and three values, each defined in lines that start with “We”.

  2. 2

    Vision and bets

    Part 1, Direction

    Where you will be in 10, 5 and 3 years. Then the approach that wins, the five core bets and the markets you serve.

  3. 3

    Goals and metrics

    Part 1, Progress

    Three annual goals, each with why it matters and three outcomes. Targets for acquisition, retention, money and team.

  4. 4

    What great looks like

    Part 2, Standards

    Each belief becomes a standard: short enough to repeat on the floor, specific enough to decide with, a number where it helps.

Every year the document is rewritten and relaunched at the company kickoff. Everyone gets access.
